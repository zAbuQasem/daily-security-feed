Splunk SOAR 8.7.0 patches multiple high-severity CVEs in third-party dependencies, including PostgreSQL (11 CVEs), PyJWT (4 CVEs), protobuf, pillow (19 CVEs), and others. The advisory covers 13 packages upgraded across cryptographic libraries, URI parsing, form handling, and image processing. While the specific CVE mechanics and impact are not detailed, this represents a significant dependency security update across both Python and Node.js ecosystems.
