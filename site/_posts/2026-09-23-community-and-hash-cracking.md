A personal narrative on the author's journey into hash cracking, covering progression from HIBP practice to community involvement with Hashcat and HashMob. Details development of custom tools (maskcat, rulecat, OpenHashAPI) in Go and Python for wordlist/rule generation and orchestration, participation in Crack Me If You Can 2024 (winning with HashMob), and creation of educational CTF framework Jabbercracky. Emphasizes cross-disciplinary skills needed for hash cracking (cryptography, hardware knowledge, data handling, custom development) and the importance of community collaboration. Primarily a community retrospective rather than technical technique deep-dive.
