Wiz researchers documented a supply chain attack targeting the arrayref Rust crate, a widely-used package for array manipulation. The attack shows operational and infrastructure overlap with known DPRK-linked threat actors, suggesting state-sponsored involvement in package ecosystem compromise. This represents a critical supply chain threat vector combining package registry compromise with nation-state actor TTPs, directly affecting Rust developers relying on this foundational dependency.
