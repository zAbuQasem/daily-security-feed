PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619) is a critical vulnerability affecting approximately 70% of websites running PHP. PortSwigger released detection capabilities within hours of disclosure, deploying a layered approach: passive checks immediately to avoid server crashes, followed by active detection probes carefully tested across multiple PHP versions and server configurations to ensure safety. Detection is now available across Burp Suite ecosystem (Professional, DAST, and Burp AT agents), enabling rapid at-scale assessment of exposure without taking systems offline. The swift response and methodical testing approach provides a case study in vulnerability detection engineering under time pressure.
