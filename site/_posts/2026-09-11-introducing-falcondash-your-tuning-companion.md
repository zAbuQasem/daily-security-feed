FalconDash is an open-sourced Azure Workbook that improves detection engineering maintenance by centralizing detection health visibility and trend analysis. The dashboard ingests alert data from Microsoft Sentinel and applies time-series analysis to identify noisy detections and tuning opportunities through pattern recognition (consistent activity, persistent onset, escalation, periodicity) and entity mapping correlation to pinpoint high-signal sources. It addresses a documented gap in detection team workflows—most lack consistent, systematic ways to act on detection performance measurements—by providing analysts and engineers a data-driven approach to prioritize tuning work and create evidence-based exclusion rules. The tool's extensibility enables integration with additional data sources and custom metadata (e.g., ServiceNow) to align with organizational workflows. FalconDash is a practical step toward proactive, systematic detection maintenance rather than reactive alert handling.
