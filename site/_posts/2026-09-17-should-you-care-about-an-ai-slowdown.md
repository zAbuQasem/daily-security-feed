Cisco Talos argues that an AI slowdown would have minimal impact on cybersecurity, given that current large language models already demonstrate strong offensive and defensive capabilities with only incremental improvements expected from newer versions. The article emphasizes that defensive AI adoption lags model improvements and that organizations remain constrained by unresolved fundamentals: asset inventories, identity and access management, least-privilege enforcement, and network segmentation. Rather than chasing model advances, organizations should invest in leveraging existing AI models more effectively and addressing the persistent "cyber-vegetables" (foundational controls) that remain the primary blocker to reducing breach risk.
