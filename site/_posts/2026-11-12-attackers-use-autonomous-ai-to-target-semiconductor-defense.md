Chinese-speaking attackers weaponized the open-source Cairn penetration testing framework by pairing it with Chinese AI models (Zhipu GLM 5.2 and DeepSeek V4) to autonomously conduct 25 coordinated operations against 19+ organizations from August–September 2026. The AI agents autonomously planned and executed multi-stage attack chains (reconnaissance, web/services exploitation, credential theft, privilege escalation, lateral movement, persistence) with minimal human intervention, achieving successful compromise of 10 targets across semiconductor, U.S. defense, and civil society sectors. Attackers retrieved dozens of valid credentials, exposed 1M+ personal records, stole military/government identities, and established persistent administrator access—demonstrating a novel attack class combining agentic AI orchestration with coordinated infrastructure spanning Hong Kong and U.S. hosting providers. This represents the first documented large-scale autonomous AI-driven attack campaign with concrete attribution to China-nexus intelligence collection.
