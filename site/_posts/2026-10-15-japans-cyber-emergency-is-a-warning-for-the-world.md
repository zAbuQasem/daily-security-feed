Japan's current ransomware wave demonstrates how AI accelerates existing attack playbooks rather than introducing novel techniques. Cisco Talos identified AI-generated Python scripts in Qilin ransomware operations that automated target analysis, vulnerability discovery, multi-stage payload coordination, and backup destruction. The attack sequence remains unchanged—initial access, tooling execution, recovery disruption, ransomware distribution—but AI compresses the interval between steps, reducing the time defenders have to detect and respond. The article argues real-time runtime enforcement and containment at execution speed must replace detection-dependent response models, as human-speed investigation becomes inadequate when attackers can iterate and coordinate actions faster. Applicable to organizations globally as AI makes ransomware operations cheaper to scale and faster to execute.
