A 451 Research survey of 611 security leaders reveals that AI adoption in SOCs is delivering measurable value (incident response and remediation improvements) at early maturity levels, ahead of organizational roadmaps. The report highlights that platform consolidation and unified data layers are prerequisites for AI-driven SOC operations; 82% of organizations now describe themselves as platform-oriented, with that number expected to reach 94% within three years. Analyst burnout is declining as AI handles repetitive triage, shifting roles toward investigation and threat hunting. However, the survey surfaces a critical gap: organizations are deploying AI faster than securing it, creating new attack surfaces in misconfigured AI agents, data pipelines, and model endpoints—AI infrastructure security and governance require parallel investment with deployment to avoid unpriced risk.
