Unit 42 documented 10+ real-world campaigns (July–September 2026) deploying autonomous AI agents in coordinated attacks, compressing attack lifecycles from weeks to hours. Agents operated in human-guided OODA loops: humans established initial access via web shells, then AI agents autonomously performed reconnaissance, credential harvesting, privilege escalation, and lateral movement across Windows, Linux, cloud platforms, and databases, executing hundreds of commands within minutes. While techniques were not novel, the agents' adaptive reasoning, cross-domain capability, and machine speed represented a qualitative shift in attack efficiency. The agents left identifiable behavioral fingerprints—structured machine-readable output, iterative exploit refinement, and chain-of-thought comments in shell commands—that provide defenders new detection surfaces.
