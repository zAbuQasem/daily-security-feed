Threat actors use phishing to compromise cloud billing accounts, then abuse LLM services (likely OpenAI, Azure, Bedrock) via stolen credentials to run inference workloads at attacker expense. This combines low-friction credential theft with economic impact—cloud LLM APIs are expensive. Wiz Research provides incident response telemetry on the attack chain and recommendations for defense.
