Microsoft's September 2026 Patch Tuesday addresses 973 vulnerabilities, including 113 marked critical, with 82 RCE flaws. Two actively exploited CVEs are disclosed: CVE-2026-81963 (Windows Update link-following EoP, 7.8) and CVE-2026-85880 (ALPC heap overflow, 7.8). High-severity flaws include CVE-2026-69730 (Windows DNS RCE via use-after-free, 9.8), CVE-2026-69676 (Kerberos auth-bypass RCE, 8.8), and CVE-2026-69854 (Spring Cloud Azure EoP via improper auth, 9.0). The advisory covers critical infrastructure services (DNS, DHCP, Kerberos, RRAS) and cloud platforms, presenting broad exploitation risk for Windows-managed environments.
