The article explores behavioral signals and detection techniques to identify rogue device joins, shifting detection focus from recognizable fingerprints of public tooling to behavioral patterns that still expose these attacks. This represents a meaningful adversary evolution in cloud identity abuse tactics and provides defenders with concrete detection guidance for Azure AD environments.
