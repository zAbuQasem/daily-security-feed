The BSI and Certitude conducted a security review of three popular children's smartwatches (One2Track Connect Neo, Garmin Bounce, Imoo Z3) using ETSI standards and the OWASP Mobile Security Testing Guide, revealing three critical categories of flaws. None of the devices enforced secure default configurations, allowing children to modify security settings, establish arbitrary Wi-Fi connections, or dial any number; two transmitted location data, messages, images, and video in plaintext—exposing children's physical location and communications to network eavesdropping. All three devices showed weak data deletion behavior, with usage data persisting locally after account deletion and no clear confirmation of removal, creating privacy risks for resold devices. Update transparency was unclear for two products, with one model already discontinued during testing and unable to receive security patches despite still being in circulation. Manufacturers acknowledged findings and committed to fixes, though the EU Cyber Resilience Act's December 2027 deadline will now enforce these baseline practices as mandatory for Class I products.
