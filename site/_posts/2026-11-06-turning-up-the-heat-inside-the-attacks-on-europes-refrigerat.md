A coordinated cyberattack campaign targeted refrigeration controllers across Europe, exploiting suspected vulnerable software versions and default credentials to gain unauthorized access and manipulate temperatures to 50°C+, destroying food inventory in 700+ supermarkets in France and expanding to 100+ locations across 10+ countries. Pro-Russian hacktivist groups NoName057(16) and Z-Pentest claimed responsibility; CISA identified authentication bypass, arbitrary code injection, and weak device configurations as exploitation vectors. Palo Alto's Unit 42 tracked 5,000+ internet-exposed vulnerable devices in top countries, with France demonstrating significant remediation progress (device exposure declined from 1,853 to 316 between October and November 2026).
