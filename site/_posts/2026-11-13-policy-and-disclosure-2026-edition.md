Google Project Zero announced a new automated vulnerability disclosure policy—"Full X"—that replaces the previous "90 + 30" standard with fixed, escalating deadlines: 90 days from report (immediately), 60 days (Feb 2027), then 30 days (Jan 2028). The policy shift responds to AI-assisted vulnerability discovery and exploitation, which have dramatically accelerated both the speed at which attackers find bugs and the time required to reverse-engineer exploits from patches, making traditional soak time ineffective. Project Zero is eliminating most special cases (grace periods, variant policy) except for active in-the-wild exploitation, public disclosure, and vendor requests. The gradual transition timeline aims to give vendors realistic remediation timelines while forcing industry-wide improvements to patch development infrastructure; severity will no longer influence disclosure decisions, only the vulnerability's availability to attackers.
