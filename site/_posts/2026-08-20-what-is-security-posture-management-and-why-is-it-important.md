Security Posture Management (SPM) is the continuous discipline of assessing, monitoring, and improving security controls across organizations' environments—spanning cloud infrastructure, SaaS applications, identities, data, and APIs. The article outlines specialized domains: CSPM (cloud infrastructure misconfigurations), SSPM (SaaS application access controls), ISPM (identity attack surface reduction), DSPM (shadow data discovery and classification), and ASPM (application risk across the software lifecycle). A key insight is that quarterly audits are insufficient; modern environments require ongoing monitoring aligned to NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0's Identify, Protect, and Detect functions to maintain situational awareness. The article provides a practical framework for understanding the fragmented attack surface across multi-cloud, multi-SaaS environments where 40% of data breaches involve data across multiple environments.
