Splunk released security advisories for Splunk Enterprise addressing four CVE groups across versions 9.4 through 10.4, with two vulnerabilities reaching CVSS 9.9 (Critical). The advisories cover improper access control (CVE-2026-76299), resource lifetime management (CVE-2026-76300), protection mechanism failure (CVE-2026-76301), and improper neutralization (CVE-2026-76302). However, the advisory provides only CWE categories and patch versions without technical detail on exploitation, attack vectors, or root causes. Patches are available across all affected versions (9.4.16, 10.0.11, 10.2.8, 10.4.4 and above).
