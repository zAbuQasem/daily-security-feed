TrustedSec provides a governance framework for responsible AI adoption in organizations. The article emphasizes classifying AI use cases by risk level (assist, retrieve, recommend, act) and establishing guardrails before deployment, including data classification rules, human review requirements, model logging, and incident response procedures. It recommends using PEST and SWOT analysis to evaluate organizational readiness and external pressures before selecting AI tools, aligning security controls with business goals and addressing identity/access, monitoring, and regulatory concerns that arise when integrating AI into business processes.
