Splunk SOAR 8.8.0 addresses multiple third-party package vulnerabilities through dependency upgrades, including 14 CVEs in PyJWT (Critical), 2 in anyio (Critical), and multiple High-severity vulnerabilities in nginx, setuptools, adm-zip, click, tornado, pyasn1, soupsieve, and others. The advisory affects Splunk SOAR versions below 8.8.0 and requires upgrade to 8.8.0 or higher for remediation. While the advisory does not provide technical details of individual vulnerabilities, it is actionable security guidance for Splunk SOAR deployments and reflects a significant supply-chain dependency patching effort.
