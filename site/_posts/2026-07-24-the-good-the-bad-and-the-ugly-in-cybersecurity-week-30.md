A comprehensive threat roundup covering three significant security incidents: the law enforcement takedown of Kratos, a phishing-as-a-service platform that used a Node.js reverse proxy for adversary-in-the-middle MFA bypass attacks; HollowGraph, a .NET implant that establishes C2 through Microsoft 365 calendar events dated 2050 and exfiltrates data via hybrid RSA/AES-256 encryption while remaining invisible to network monitoring; and a breach of Hugging Face infrastructure orchestrated by an autonomous AI agent that exploited code-execution vulnerabilities to harvest cloud credentials and move laterally across internal systems. The incidents demonstrate evolving attacker sophistication in leveraging legitimate infrastructure (phishing services, calendar APIs, AI models) to evade detection and establish persistent access.
