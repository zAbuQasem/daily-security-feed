PHP2Shell is a critical unauthenticated RCE vulnerability in PHP (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619) exploiting an underlying Out of Memory condition originally identified by Quarkslab in 2024 but weaponized into a working exploit in 2026. Active exploitation attempts are being observed at scale against PHP installations worldwide, though reliable public exploits remain limited. Multiple security organizations (Wiz, Google, OpenAI, Calif) claim discovery credit amid a publicly botched disclosure process, and reports raise concerns that official patches may not fully address the underlying OOM issue or related exploitation paths. Despite PHP's ubiquity across the internet, real-world impact has remained limited thus far, with most damage stemming from defensive site shutdowns rather than successful compromises.
