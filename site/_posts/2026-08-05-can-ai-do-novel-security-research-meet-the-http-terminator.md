HTTP Terminator is an autonomous AI system for discovering novel HTTP desync (request smuggling) vulnerabilities. The system generates hypotheses, evaluates them against live websites via authorized bug bounty programs, weaponizes successful findings into exploitable vulnerabilities, then uses each discovery to fuel further research in a cascade effect. AI-discovered techniques successfully compromised banks, security solutions, and government infrastructure through novel desync triggers, desync patterns, and Response Queue Poisoning variants. Research includes undisclosed reconnaissance techniques and hints at entirely new attack classes, demonstrating AI's capability for genuinely novel vulnerability discovery when guided by domain expertise. The tool will be open-sourced; research presented at Black Hat USA 2026 and DEF CON 34.
