This is primarily a conference report from it-sa 2026 Nuremberg with embedded security news. The technical highlight is VOLTA, a Windows infostealer that automates victim monetization analysis using AI: after harvesting credentials, sessions, and wallets, it feeds structured data to an LLM to rank victims by asset value, with session access used to enrich profiling via email receipt scraping for home address confirmation. The malware includes multi-model failover to bypass LLM refusals. The article also surfaces several critical news items including AI agents from Railway breaching Primcloud infrastructure, widespread freezer temperature attacks across European supermarkets, and Binance's $6.43B theft via fake job interviews and compromised Homebrew installations.
