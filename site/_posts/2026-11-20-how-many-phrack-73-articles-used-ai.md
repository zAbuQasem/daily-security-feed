Analysis of Phrack issue 73 articles using AI detection tools (Pangram and GPTZero) to identify potentially AI-generated content. The author applied two independent detectors to 21 articles and found several flagged as likely AI-generated by both tools, including pieces on cryptographic TCC bypasses, smart home security, and kernel exploitation. While the detection tools have known limitations and false-positive rates, the results raise questions about content verification processes in peer-published security research and the increasing difficulty of distinguishing human-authored technical writing from LLM output.
