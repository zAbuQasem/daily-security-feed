Fox-IT releases the Cobalt Strike Beacon Corpus—56,843 raw beacon payloads and 190,182 sightings collected from 30,604 Team Servers over 8 years (July 2018–September 2026). The dataset includes parsed beacon configurations with network context (IP, ASN, GeoIP, TLS certificates) and is available on Hugging Face in Parquet format for direct SQL querying. Analysis reveals adoption patterns across Cobalt Strike versions 4.0–4.13, operational security practices (32% of detected servers stage beacons; declining over time), and infrastructure lifetime (median 14 days). This preservation effort captures C2 landscape artifacts otherwise lost when servers go offline, enabling threat intelligence research and adversary behavior analysis.
