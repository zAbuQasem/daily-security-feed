Palo Alto Unit 42 examines seasonal cyber threats targeting retail during peak 2026 holiday shopping, highlighting ransomware campaigns by TGR-CRI-1173, Spikey Scorpius, and Howling Scorpius that claimed 565 retail victims year-to-date, data extortion attacks by Bling Libra and Hazy Scorpius, DDoS campaigns using botnets like DDoSia to flood APIs and e-commerce checkouts, and digital skimming via CVE-2024-34102 (CosmicSting) that injects malicious payment scripts into checkout flows. The research emphasizes operational vulnerabilities—code freezes and reduced staffing during peak season—enable attackers, and recommends zero-trust architecture, data loss prevention, rate-limiting, and continuous vulnerability assessment to mitigate impact on customer trust and uptime.
