Sygnia's analysis of crypto-specific attack patterns and threat landscape, grounded in a real $12M+ breach of cryptocurrency exchange CryptoCo. The attack chain begins with a fake LinkedIn recruiter delivering credential-stealing malware, captures AWS access tokens, escalates through EC2 and EKS lateral movement, and culminates in 40 unauthorized transactions across five cryptocurrencies. The article emphasizes that crypto's immediate transaction finality creates unique urgency: compromise-to-loss occurs within seconds or blocks, with no reversal mechanism. Defenses require four integrated domains: identity governance, infrastructure isolation, custody/transaction authorization separation, and on-chain/off-chain detection linked to programmatic controls, not just visibility.
