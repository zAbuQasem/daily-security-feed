Weekly security digest covering multiple incidents: TeamPCP compromised open-source projects (Trivy, KICS, LiteLLM) via stolen developer credentials to distribute backdoored updates; NovaCookies is a subscription phishing platform operating as an Adversary-in-the-Middle proxy that spoofs DocuSign notifications, embeds malicious links below security gateway inspection layers, and uses OAuth error-redirect techniques to relay credentials and MFA codes in real-time to phishing infrastructure; Spark RAT campaigns target Cambodia using multi-stage infection chains that abuse legitimate Tencent applications for DLL side-loading, perform anti-sandbox timing checks, decrypt shellcode from PNG files, and achieve persistence via service registration or process injection into vssvc.exe; parallel law enforcement operations dismantled West African cybercrime networks, seized Chinese state-sponsored IoT botnet C2 infrastructure, and sanctioned Iran-linked entities for critical infrastructure breaches.
