Cisco Talos and Recorded Future jointly tracked UAT-6275 (TAG-193/Silent Lynx), a state-sponsored espionage APT targeting CIS government organizations since June 2025. The group deploys multiple infection chains combining macro-enabled documents, PS2EXE downloaders, GitHub-hosted PowerShell scripts, and WinRAR archives exploiting CVE-2025-8088, delivering custom reverse shells for interactive access. Victims span nine countries with strategic focus on diplomatic entities, energy infrastructure (Turkmennebit, Orsk refinery), and water resource management—interests tied to regional geopolitics and the Russia–Ukraine war. Critical OpSec failures exposed three public staging directories and a GitHub repository revealing payload builds, tool inventories, C2 infrastructure changes, and preparation for post-compromise operations including Linux targeting. Medium-confidence attribution to Kazakhstan based on infrastructure, language artifacts, working hours, and regional targeting patterns.
