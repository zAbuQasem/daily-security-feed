Talos Intelligence newsletter summarizing Q2 2026 incident response trends, highlighting phishing as the driver of over half of all engagements, with attackers using QR codes and ARToken to bypass MFA. The report notes ransomware operators increasingly weaponizing legitimate remote management tools (MeshAgent, Zoho Assist) for persistent access, targeting healthcare and public administration with emphasis on zero-tolerance-downtime sectors. Recommended defenses include transitioning to phishing-resistant MFA (FIDO2/hardware keys), behavior-based monitoring for unauthorized administrative tools, and centralized logging with 90+ day retention. Brief headlines cover Minnesota water system coordinated attacks, SOHO router DNS manipulation for credential harvesting, Azure Automation cross-tenant identity takeover misconfiguration, and Check Point SmartConsole authentication bypass PoC.
