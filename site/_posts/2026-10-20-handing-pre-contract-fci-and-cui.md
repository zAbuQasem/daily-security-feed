TrustedSec clarifies widespread confusion around FCI (Federal Contract Information) and CUI (Controlled Unclassified Information) safeguarding obligations during government contract solicitation phases. The critical finding: contractors have NO compliance obligations to safeguard FCI/CUI before a contract is signed unless they enter an explicit pre-contract agreement—neither FAR 52.204-21, DFARS, nor CMMC apply during the pre-contract phase. The CMMC program's rollout has amplified misconceptions, leading many prime contractors to incorrectly impose CMMC compliance requirements on potential subcontractors during bidding when no such government requirement exists. The article explains that compliance obligations are agreement-specific and vary by agency and solicitation; contractors relying on previous contracts or regulatory assumptions to enforce requirements on subcontractors are acting beyond actual government scope. This guidance is essential for government contractors and subcontractors to avoid overreaching compliance burdens and for those responding to RFPs to push back with accurate legal positions.
