Cisco Talos reports 90 ransomware incidents in Japan during H1 2026 (4.7% increase YoY), with The Gentlemen emerging as the most active group (14 incidents). Talos identified open directory infrastructure attributed to The Gentlemen containing ransomware samples for ESXi/Windows, RustHound (Rust-based Active Directory enumeration for BloodHound attack path analysis), and exploitation code targeting CVE-2025-2479 (SQL injection for unauthorized database manipulation). Qilin, the second most active ransomware group in 2026, is leveraging AI to improve operational efficiency. The threat predominantly targets small- and medium-sized enterprises (<¥1B capitalization, 80% of victims), with manufacturing as the top affected sector (34% of incidents). The Gentlemen's leak site activity scaled 2.2x from January to July 2026, with evidence suggesting Russian-speaking actors' involvement.
