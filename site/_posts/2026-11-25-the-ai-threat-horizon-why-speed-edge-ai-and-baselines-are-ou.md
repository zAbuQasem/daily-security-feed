Palo Alto Unit42 reports observations of AI-driven threat actors executing coordinated attacks at machine speed, generating 117 unique scripts and executing 8,000+ commands in 12 days—compared to dozens of commands by human attackers over similar periods. The behavioral signature is distinct: AI agents operate flawlessly with 1.4-second command intervals versus 24-second human timing, with zero syntax errors. The core defense requirement is shifting to millisecond-level detection and automated response via edge AI (endpoint and NGFW-based), baseline anomaly detection, and cross-platform telemetry fusion to catch reconnaissance activity before data exfiltration or encryption. Traditional security tools remain relevant but must operate in automated, real-time mode rather than human-paced investigation workflows.
