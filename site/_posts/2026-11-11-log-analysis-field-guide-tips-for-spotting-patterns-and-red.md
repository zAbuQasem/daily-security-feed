A practical guide to log analysis fundamentals for SOC analysts and threat hunters, covering the five core log elements (timestamp, source, event type, user/actor, outcome) and how to extract patterns from them. The article outlines key red flags including failed-then-successful logins (password guessing), after-hours admin activity, impossible travel scenarios (credential compromise indicators), unexpected account modifications, and unusual processes or connections. Emphasizes contextual analysis—knowing normal behavior to spot deviations—and provides a filtering strategy to reduce noise rather than analyze exhaustively. Includes investigation methodology and pointers to datasets (OTRF, EVTX-ATTACK-SAMPLES) and frameworks (MITRE ATT&CK) for learning log sources tied to specific attack techniques.
