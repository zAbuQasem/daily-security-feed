A BSides Frankfurt technical talk deconstructing modern macOS initial access chains used by threat actors. The presentation covers the full attack pipeline: infection vectors (malvertising, ClickFix campaigns, deceptive ads), execution methods (Bash, Python, AppleScript, JXA, Perl), and delivery mechanisms (trojanized Mach-O binaries, Platypus-packaged apps, Electron frameworks, and poisoned DMG/PKG installers). The talk provides real-world attacker methodologies and threat hunting strategies for defending against macOS-focused malware campaigns. Video forthcoming on dfir.ch.
