Talos explores a sophisticated social engineering tactic where threat actors pose as consultants offering lucrative but suspiciously plausible fees ($300/hour) to screen targets for valuable access or knowledge. The attack chain progresses from initial phone consultation screening to commissioned written reports, then requests for 'special reports' containing non-public insights — forcing targets to abuse internal access, probe systems, or exploit professional relationships to complete the assignment. The article also highlights CAIRN, Talos' new open-source toolkit for hunting AI-integrated malware using metadata-first analysis to extract cognitive artifacts (prompt templates, API keys, jailbreak terms) without binary reverse engineering, addressing the rapid evolution of autonomous AI-driven malware variants.
