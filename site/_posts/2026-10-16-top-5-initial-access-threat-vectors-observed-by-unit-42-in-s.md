Unit 42's September 2026 threat intelligence report ranks the top five initial access vectors used by adversaries: (1) exploitation of public-facing applications and perimeter services remains the primary entry mechanism, (2) credential abuse and spray attacks continue as a leading vector often chained with edge vulnerabilities, (3) phishing and social engineering remain prevalent against public sector and critical infrastructure, (4) supply chain compromise delivers cascading impact across interconnected ecosystems, and (5) drive-by compromises via watering holes operate as automated exploitation techniques requiring minimal user interaction. The report reflects a clear adversary shift toward external-facing infrastructure targeting and identity compromise as primary operational access paths.
