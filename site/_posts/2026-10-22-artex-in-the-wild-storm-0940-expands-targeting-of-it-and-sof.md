Wiz Research identified ARTEX, an AI agent deployed by the Storm-0940 threat actor, actively targeting IT and software vendors through lateral movement and credential acquisition against Okta, Azure Entra, and AWS Cognito. The researchers recovered forensic evidence from audit logs showing the attack progression and provided detection indicators. This represents an active, ongoing threat to SaaS and cloud infrastructure critical to software supply chains.
