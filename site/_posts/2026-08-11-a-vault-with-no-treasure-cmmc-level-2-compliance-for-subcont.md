A technical guide to CMMC Level 2 compliance scoping for defense subcontractors that don't handle Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI). The article clarifies that CMMC Level 2 audit requirements should only apply to subcontracts explicitly flowing down CUI; many contractors incorrectly impose these requirements on subcontractors handling only Federal Contract Information (FCI). TrustedSec explains the contractual flow-down rules under FAR 52.204-21 and DFARS 252.204-7012 and provides practical designs for minimal 'CUI enclaves'—including air-gapped workstations, cloud-isolated systems, and virtual desktop access models—that allow subcontractors to pass C3PAO certification while minimizing unnecessary compliance overhead. The post emphasizes scoping boundaries and the role of External Service Providers, with guidance on distinguishing FCI (Level 1) from CUI (Level 2) requirements.
