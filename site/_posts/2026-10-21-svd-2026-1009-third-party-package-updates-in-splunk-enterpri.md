Splunk released critical security updates addressing 26+ CVEs across third-party dependencies in Splunk Enterprise: Go compiler (13 CVEs), golang-grpc, and golang.org/x crypto/net packages used by postgres and MongoDB utilities; plus Node.js dependencies in the dashboard enterprise interface. Fixes are available in Splunk Enterprise 9.4.16, 10.0.11, 10.2.8, and 10.4.4 or higher. The advisory provides version mappings and affected components but omits technical details on individual vulnerabilities or exploitation paths.
