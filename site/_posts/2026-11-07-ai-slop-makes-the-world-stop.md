A thoughtful analysis of organizational risks arising from rushed AI adoption without adequate expertise. The author argues that enterprises optimizing for adoption metrics and headcount reduction rather than outcomes create a dangerous pattern: replacing critical staff with broad AI access, then deploying unskilled personnel to build security-critical systems (e.g., Claude building employee portals with payroll data exposed via admin:admin credentials). The piece draws historical parallels to cloud migration hype, showing how vendor lock-in, cost escalation, and false economy-of-scale arguments mirror current AI adoption pressures. While not technical vulnerability research, it identifies a real attack surface: organizations that eliminate security judgment and operational understanding become vulnerable to the inevitable breaches that follow premature, uncontrolled AI deployment.
