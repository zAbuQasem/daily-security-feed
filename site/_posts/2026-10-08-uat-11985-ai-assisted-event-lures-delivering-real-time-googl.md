Cisco Talos documented an advanced APT spear-phishing campaign targeting Taiwan research organizations that demonstrates AI-assisted template-based email generation at scale, combined with QR code phishing (quishing) on event posters. The threat actor deployed a sophisticated adversary-in-the-middle (AitM) phishing framework impersonating Google authentication with a hybrid HTTP/WebSocket architecture to intercept credentials and MFA challenges in real time. Social engineering leveraged institutional impersonation, publicly-available event details for legitimacy laundering, and personalized flattery based on recipient professional profiles scraped from public sources. The phishing kit analysis suggests a Simplified Chinese-speaking developer, indicating potential state-sponsored or organized criminal infrastructure. The campaign demonstrates emerging threat patterns: AI-accelerated customization for at-scale social engineering, multi-vector delivery (email plus physical world QR codes), and real-time MFA defeat capabilities.
