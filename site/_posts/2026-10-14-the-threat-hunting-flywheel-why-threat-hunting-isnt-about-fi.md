Black Hills InfoSec outlines the threat hunting flywheel—a continuous improvement framework where each hunt feeds back into the security program. The article describes four steps: hypothesis-driven or baseline-driven focus selection, iterative investigation, discovery (whether positive findings or validation of working defenses), and knowledge refinement through detection rules, baseline documentation, and hypothesis generation. The core insight is that threat hunting's value multiplies over 12–18 months as patterns become obvious and investigation approaches become routine, transforming reactive hunting into a self-improving defensive system.
